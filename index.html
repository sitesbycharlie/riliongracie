01 — ABOUT THE ACADEMY
Built on years of dedicated training, strong lineage, and a commitment to making high-quality martial arts accessible to everyone.

Head instructor Bryan Gorman trained for almost a decade under Brian Coyle before travelling to Miami in 2010 to train directly with Rilion Gracie and Professor Romulo Reis Pereira — a connection to the Gracie lineage that shapes every class taught here.

Whether you're after practical self-defence, better fitness, more confidence, stress relief, or competition, our BJJ gym offers a welcoming environment for complete beginners through to experienced practitioners.
